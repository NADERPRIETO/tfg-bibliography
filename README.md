# tfg-bibliography
All the papers I screened and/or read during my university final project. Making this repo purely for traceability purposes.

I made a PRISMA systematic review as a part of my final project. The PRISMA methodology entails, among other things, reviewing the materials **twice** before deciding whether the data they contain is relevant or not: first through *screening* and then through *full-text screening*. Both of those stages result in several (ideally, most) of the materials being discarded, so in the end only a few of the original ones are fit for *data extraction*.

In this case, screening consisted in reading only the title and abstract of each article and full-text screening consisted in reading the whole thing.

The articles that were screened are listed in the screened.csv file. The articles that were *full-text screened* (aka read) are listed in the read.csv file.

Data in both of those is separated by semicolons because Spain.

screened.csv columns are: Title, DOI or other identifier (PMID, ISBN), Result (included, excluded due to citerium 1, excluded due to criterium 2)

read.csv columns are: Title, Studied signal (ECG, EEG, etc), Application (medicine, research, industry, etc), Goal of the study (to contribute to the knowledge on a biomedical signal, to test a new measurement system, etc)
